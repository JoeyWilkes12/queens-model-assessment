## Design review

Before any new paid inference, GPT-6.1 Sol Xhigh independently reviewed the bounded design and recomputed its reservation: 18 GPT calls × $0.132304 + 6 Claude calls × $0.230320 + 12 Gemini calls × $0.044320 = **$4.295232**. The new $5 alone covers the plan; the prior study's remainder is not used.

The reviewer required fresh original-image controls and representation counterbalancing, not merely balanced provider order. The frozen schedule uses all six representation permutations across the six selected pairs in repetition 1 and reverses each order in repetition 2. This is a deterministic counterbalanced design, **not randomized allocation**. It follows the purpose of blocking/counterbalancing described in [NIST experimental-design guidance](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pri/section3/pri332.htm), without claiming the randomization properties of a randomized block design.

The reviewer approved descriptive 0/2, 1/2 and 2/2 reporting, rejected stable-accuracy or isolated image-perception claims, and required all model outcomes to be retained. The failed-cell selection is explicit. No family-level model ranking or statistical significance is inferred. Source-selection and grading are auditable separately from model interpretation.
